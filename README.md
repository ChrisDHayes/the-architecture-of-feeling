# The Architecture of Feeling
### Depeche Mode, Decoded · Christopher Hayes

An interactive research exhibit about Depeche Mode's sonic identity: what can be measured, how recordings relate acoustically, and what those measurements cannot establish about how music feels.

### What the evidence supports

- The acoustic analysis covers **147 provisional recording candidates**, with source-linked feature values, interactive inspection, and exploratory acoustic clustering. The source files are **not independently verified studio masters**.
- The outer radial listening-reach layer uses **144 ListenBrainz counts linked to the selected recording IDs** and **three explicitly identified alternate-album-version proxies**. It can display unique listeners, total listens, or no overlay; these counts **do not enter acoustic clustering** and are not global audience estimates.
- Hover or focus on a measured scatter point to inspect its exact feature values; click, tap or select by keyboard for a persistent source receipt with comparisons to the displayed cohort.
- **Personal favourites** and the distinct **Home** finale remain. Unmeasured favourites are not assigned fictional acoustic coordinates.
- Acoustic measurements, exploratory model-based similarity, listening activity, and personal interpretation are deliberately kept distinct. Older illustrative/synthetic content in the exhibit remains identified as such.

**Author:** Christopher Hayes

## Open offline

Open `index.html` in a modern browser. No server, media, third-party library, or network request is required for the visual narrative. The official Depeche Mode video link opens YouTube and requires internet access.

## What changed

- The previous *But Not Tonight* final chapter has been replaced with **HOME — A Pattern of Return**.
- The Cartography of Home is an original SVG interactive memory map: six eras/relationships converge on an abstract centre. Users can select memories, use keyboard controls, or **Gather the Threads**.
- The closing dedication returns to Depeche Mode, the music, and the author's family.
- A contextual link opens Depeche Mode's official **Home** music video at https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DZHs-SRJbzU.
- A separate measured-data strip reports *Home*'s archived candidate submission: tempo estimate 151.128799438 BPM, spectral centroid 1016.85058594 Hz, onset rate 4.22934579849 per second. Source MusicBrainz recording MBID `42f01cf7-2fdc-4f22-bf08-58e791ffdb22`, AcousticBrainz low-level submission, codec MP3 320 kbps, Essentia 2.1-beta2. Feature parity was verified by the source retrieval project; album master identity and comparability remain unverified. These values do **not** drive the personal-memory map.
- The prior 167-entry catalogue and 148 provisional source records are preserved; the measured chapter, existing SVG exhibits and story content were not changed.
- Locked V2.9 remains separate and unchanged.

## Evidence and interpretation contract

The 6 memory nodes and paths are authored representations of lived experience, **not** positions derived from audio features, model outputs, musical similarity, or emotional classifiers. They must not be described as measured relationships. No copyrighted music or lyrics are distributed. The video is opened on the band's official YouTube channel rather than hosted locally.

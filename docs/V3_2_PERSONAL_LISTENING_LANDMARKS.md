# V3.2 personal listening landmarks

The **Movement and brightness. Not mood.** scatterplot now distinguishes **measured acoustic evidence** from the author's **personal favourites**. This is an editorial annotation only: it does not alter the recorded feature vectors, default axes, source identities, acoustic clustering, tree distances, playlist statistics or research scope.

## Visual contract

- **Home** remains the larger red anchor, even when another song is selected.
- Eight other favourites are represented by smaller gold outlines around real source-derived acoustic scatter points, plus clickable shortcuts beneath the field.
- The optional **Hide favourites** control turns off gold/red outlines and the shortcuts while leaving the acoustic coordinates intact. Restoring the overlay does not change source values or axes.
- The existing song selector, source provenance, tooltip, tree and signature panel continue to work. Clicking a shortcut opens that same song in the existing source inspector.

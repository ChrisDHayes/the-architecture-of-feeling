Our evidence contract

Measured audio descriptors, computational inferences, and human listening interpretations must remain distinct. An AcousticBrainz submission is not proof that its underlying file matches a chosen studio-album edition or master. Similarity, mastering effects and catalogue-wide evolution remain unproven at this stage.

This project preserves the approved V2.9 interactive creative edition unchanged. The 25-song research selection is a separate working registry and may differ from the editorial exhibition.

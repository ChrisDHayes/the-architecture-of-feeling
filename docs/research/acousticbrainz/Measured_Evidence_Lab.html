04 / Validation boundaries

Evidence before inference.

Measured: Ten descriptive source fields, their units, album/title tags, source codec and Essentia build, reconciled to the original raw low-level JSON.

Not established: exact master, uniform extraction conditions, similarity, affective labels, chronology effects, or listener experiences. A lossless submission is not a verified master.

Next gate: Resolve the missing album versions, validate recording/master boundaries, assess compression and extractor sensitivity, and pre-register the normalization and clustering methodology before producing a measured dendrogram.

PROLOGUE / WHY THIS EXISTS

Before the data,
there was a feeling.

Depeche Mode became the soundtrack to my adolescence before I had words for everything I was going through. Changing schools. New relationships. Broken hearts. Those strange years of trying to find where I belonged. The songs didn't give me answers, but they made room for what I was feeling.

I was fourteen when I first saw them in Toronto in 1988. Decades later, I've stood beside my wife at more Depeche Mode shows than I can count, and every time I'm moved to tears. Now I share this music with my son. I think part of what I'm sharing is the person I was before he knew me.

Some music reminds you of a particular moment. Depeche Mode holds entire chapters of my life. This project began as a curiosity about what makes their sound so distinctive. Beneath that is a more personal question: how can sound stay with us, change with us, and mean something new each time we return to it?

EPILOGUE / WHAT ENDURES

The music kept
coming back.

I was fourteen when I first saw Depeche Mode at Toronto’s CNE Stadium in 1988. I saw them there again in 1990, and I have returned for every Toronto concert since. Across all those years, the songs changed—and so did I.

I wanted to know whether a band whose sound kept evolving had an architecture I could recognize in data. We can trace rhythm, timbre, similarity and change; we can ask listeners what they feel. Yet a model cannot fully describe why a song becomes inseparable from a place, a night, or a memory. This is the distance between evidence and experience.

Data might help us see the architecture.
It cannot fully account for the feeling.

ACT VIII / THE EVIDENCE CONTRACT

Built to be challenged.

This edition distinguishes catalogue facts, illustrative synthetic sound, externally supplied measurements when available, computational predictions, and interpretations. This is what separates a data story from a decorative dashboard.

01

What is documented

15 studio albums, real track titles and album associations from the V1.5 catalogue. Original editions and variant tracks remain distinct.

02

What is simulated

In default mode, all audio features, similarity distances, waveform, emotional axes and flowfield values. Imported values are externally supplied and identified by contract, never silently certified.

03

What is missing

A complete, independently checked and version-matched real acoustic dataset. A partial dataset can be staged but will not alter catalogue-wide claims.

THE EASIER ROUTE TO REAL MUSIC DATA

One small local test—rather than a huge archive.

A practical next iteration can extract basic audio descriptors locally from a few files that you can legitimately access, with no upload and no GitHub Actions workflow. An open historical feature dataset remains an optional expansion—not the project's bottleneck.

Browser-based audio processing can use the Web Audio API; proper feature extraction and copyright review are separate implementation gates. This preview only plays local files.

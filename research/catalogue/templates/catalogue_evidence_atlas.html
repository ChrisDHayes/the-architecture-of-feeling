Before we ask how songs relate, what do we actually know?

167 catalogue entries. One curated 25-song research baseline. An expanded search of 142 more tracks. This atlas maps **where historical acoustic evidence exists**, the quality of the available recordings, and the gaps that remain.

Read this before exploring: A retrieved recording is not a verified album master. No colour or placement below represents musical similarity, emotional meaning, or song quality. The research preview does not modify the locked V2.9 exhibition.

Act III / evidence discipline

What we have measured is not everything we want to know.

AcousticBrainz precomputed features were extracted from independently submitted audio files. Source titles, album tags, MusicBrainz IDs, codec flags and extractor versions can support a provisional identification; they do not establish the exact studio master. Tracks from 2023 are outside the archive's submission period.

The initial 25-song research dataset is deliberately kept separate here. The atlas does not combine heterogeneous measurements into a normalized catalogue or infer emotion, influence, historical change, or causal production effects.
